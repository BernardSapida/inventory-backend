
 Italian Pizzas

1. Pizza Margherita

Ingredients
1 pizza dough
½ cup tomato sauce
200 g fresh mozzarella, sliced
Fresh basil leaves
2 tbsp olive oil
Salt to taste

Step-by-Step Recipe

Preheat the oven to 250°C (480°F).
Roll out the pizza dough.
Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
Add mozzarella slices.
Bake for 10–12 minutes until the crust is golden.
Top with fresh basil.
Drizzle with olive oil and serve.

25. Pizza Calzone (folded pizza)

Ingredients

1 pizza dough
150 g mozzarella
100 g ricotta cheese
100 g ham or salami
2 tbsp tomato sauce
1 egg (for egg wash)
Olive oil

Step-by-Step Recipe

Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
Roll the dough into a large circle.
Spread tomato sauce on one half of the dough.
Add mozzarella, ricotta, and ham.
Fold the dough over to form a half-moon shape.
Seal the edges by pressing with a fork.
Brush with beaten egg.
Bake for 18–20 minutes until golden brown.
Let cool slightly before serving.

26. Spaghetti Carbonara

Ingredients

400 g spaghetti
150 g pancetta or guanciale, diced
2 eggs
2 egg yolks
100 g Pecorino Romano, grated
Black pepper

Step-by-Step Recipe

Cook the spaghetti until al dente.
Fry the pancetta until crispy.
Whisk together the eggs, yolks, Pecorino, and black pepper.
Drain the pasta, reserving a little pasta water.
Toss the pasta with the pancetta.
Remove from the heat and stir in the egg mixture.
Add a little pasta water if needed to create a creamy sauce.
Serve with extra Pecorino and black pepper.

28. Spaghetti alle Vongole

Ingredients

400 g spaghetti
1 kg fresh clams, cleaned
4 garlic cloves, sliced
¼ cup olive oil
½ cup dry white wine
Fresh parsley
Salt and pepper

Step-by-Step Recipe

Cook the spaghetti until al dente.
Heat olive oil and sauté the garlic.
Add the clams and pour in the white wine.
Cover and cook until the clams open.
Discard any unopened clams.
Toss the cooked spaghetti into the pan.
Mix well with the clam juices.
Garnish with parsley and serve.

31. Penne alla Vodka

Ingredients

400 g penne
2 tbsp olive oil
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 garlic cloves, minced
120 ml vodka
500 g crushed tomatoes
120 ml heavy cream
50 g Parmesan cheese, grated
Salt and black pepper
Fresh basil (optional)

Step-by-Step Recipe

Cook the penne according to the package directions until al dente.
Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
Sauté the onion until softened, then add the garlic and cook for 1 minute.
Pour in the vodka and simmer for 2–3 minutes.
Add the crushed tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes.
Stir in the heavy cream and Parmesan cheese.
Season with salt and black pepper.
Toss the cooked penne in the sauce.
Garnish with fresh basil and serve.

35. Lasagne alla Bolognese

Ingredients

12 lasagne sheets
500 g ground beef
500 g crushed tomatoes
1 onion, chopped
2 garlic cloves, minced
500 ml béchamel sauce
200 g mozzarella, shredded
100 g Parmesan cheese, grated
2 tbsp olive oil
Salt and black pepper

Step-by-Step Recipe

Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
Cook the ground beef with the onion and garlic.
Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 20 minutes.
Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
Add lasagne sheets, béchamel sauce, mozzarella, and Parmesan.
Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
Finish with cheese on top.
Bake for 40–45 minutes.
Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

40. Cacio e Pepe

Ingredients

400 g spaghetti or tonnarelli
100 g Pecorino Romano, finely grated
2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
120 ml reserved pasta water

Step-by-Step Recipe

Cook the pasta until al dente.
Reserve the pasta water before draining.
Toast the black pepper in a dry pan for 1 minute.
Add a small amount of pasta water to the pan.
Toss in the cooked pasta.
Gradually add the Pecorino Romano while stirring continuously.
Add more pasta water as needed until a creamy sauce forms.
Serve immediately with extra black pepper.

50. Pasta e Fagioli

Ingredients

250 g small pasta (ditalini or elbow macaroni)
2 cans (400 g each) cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
1 onion, diced
2 carrots, diced
2 celery stalks, diced
2 garlic cloves, minced
800 ml vegetable or chicken broth
400 g crushed tomatoes
2 tbsp olive oil
1 tsp Italian seasoning
Salt and black pepper
Parmesan cheese (optional)

Step-by-Step Recipe

Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
Sauté the onion, carrots, celery, and garlic until softened.
Stir in the crushed tomatoes, broth, and Italian seasoning.
Add the beans and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
Stir in the pasta and cook until tender.
Season with salt and black pepper.
Serve hot with grated Parmesan cheese if desired.
Garnish with fresh parsley or a drizzle of olive oil for extra flavor.
